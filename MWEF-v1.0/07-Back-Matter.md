## GLOSSARY

**AI Build Package** — The consolidated, implementation-ready specification produced at Stage Gate 10 that a developer or AI code-generation model uses to construct the actual website.

**AI Orchestrator** — The human role (Volume I, Sec. 2.2) responsible for managing AI model handoffs, prompt quality, and verification of AI-produced output against Stage Gate standards.

**Benchmark Validation** — The Stage Gate 7.5 activity that stress-tests the winning design direction against fixed performance, accessibility, and technical SEO standards before executive approval.

**Blueprint** — See *Master Website Blueprint*.

**Compliance Constraint Log** — The running record, seeded at Stage Gate 1 and expanded through later gates, of all regulatory and licensing requirements applicable to the client's content and advertising.

**Compliance Liaison** — The client-side individual responsible for reviewing and approving all rate, disclosure, licensing, and advertising-claim content. MWEF firms and their consultants do not issue compliance sign-off themselves.

**Core Web Vitals (CWV)** — Google's user-experience performance metrics: Largest Contentful Paint (LCP), Interaction to Next Paint (INP), and Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS), used throughout this manual as binding performance targets.

**Decision Authority** — The named individual(s), specified in the Project Charter, with final sign-off power at each approval gate.

**Decision Register** — The append-only ledger of every material engagement decision, its rationale, and its alternatives considered (Volume I, Sec. 4).

**Design Tournament** — The Stage Gate 7.5 activity in which 2–3 fully realized design directions are scored head-to-head on a fixed rubric rather than a single design being presented for approval or rejection.

**Engagement Lead** — The consultant with overall accountability for engagement outcomes and Decision Register authority.

**Entity SEO** — An SEO approach focused on establishing unambiguous, machine-readable identity for an organization, its people, and its products/services — the foundation of both classic knowledge-panel visibility and AI-mediated search citation.

**Exit Criteria** — The pass/fail conditions that must be met before an engagement can move from one Stage Gate to the next.

**Future-Proofing Review** — The Stage Gate 7.5 activity evaluating whether the approved design system will scale cleanly to a defined future growth scenario.

**GenerateBlocks / GenerateBlocks Pro** — The WordPress page-building plugin used as the default component/pattern-building layer in the MWEF technology stack.

**GeneratePress / GeneratePress Premium** — The WordPress theme framework used as the default visual/structural foundation in the MWEF technology stack.

**Knowledge Base (KB)** — The persistent, versioned, structured store of every artifact and decision produced during an engagement (Volume I, Sec. 5).

**LLM Handoff Protocol** — The four-layer context package (Charter, History, State, Task) used to brief any AI model working on an MWEF engagement (Volume I, Sec. 9).

**Master Website Blueprint (MWB)** — The single living document representing the current, approved state of the site's architecture, design, and content (Volume I, Sec. 6).

**NMLS** — Nationwide Multistate Licensing System, the registry of record for mortgage lender and loan officer licensing in the United States.

**Positioning Statement** — The single, client-approved sentence defining how the client is distinctly positioned relative to its competitive set (Stage Gate 3).

**Project Charter** — The foundational governance document defining scope, objectives, roles, and decision authority for an engagement (Volume I, Sec. 3).

**Project Memory** — The rolling summary of current engagement status, distinct from the full Decision Register and the current-state Blueprint (Volume I, Sec. 10).

**Rank Math** — The default SEO plugin used in the MWEF technology stack for metadata, schema, and technical SEO management.

**Stage Gate** — A defined phase of the MWEF methodology with fixed inputs, outputs, roles, and exit criteria; the fundamental unit of engagement structure.

**Topical Cluster Model** — The pillar-page/cluster-page content architecture used to build genuine topical authority (Stage Gate 5).

**White Space Opportunity Map** — The Stage Gate 2 output identifying borrower needs no competitor currently serves well.

### Appendix C — Mandatory Compliance Checkpoints Quick Reference

Compliance Liaison sign-off is **mandatory and non-waivable** at: Stage Gate 8 (Compliance Content Checklist), Stage Gate 9 (page-by-page Compliance Clearance Log), and Stage Gate 11 (final site-wide Compliance Sign-Off Record). No Decision Authority, client urgency, or schedule pressure overrides these three checkpoints.
