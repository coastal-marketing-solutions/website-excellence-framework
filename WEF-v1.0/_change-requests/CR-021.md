# CR-021 — Page-count enumeration + coverage-check + two-approval retrospectives

**Approved By:** Methodology Governance Board

Four Engagement Retrospective Register entries from a live real estate (Distressed Property Advocate / Cash Home Buyer blend) engagement's copywriting and build-package stages. **RETRO-013:** a sitemap's total page count, first stated as a bare number, propagated uncorrected across four Stage Gates because nothing forced recomputation from the sitemap itself; the eventual correction pass introduced a *second* miscount because it was a spot-recount rather than an exhaustive category-by-category re-enumeration. **RETRO-014:** an earlier stage's own "100% sitemap coverage" claim was silently wrong because its coverage check verified templates against its own internal category list rather than the sitemap's actual categories, letting an entire non-marketing-priority page category (compliance/legal) go undetected as missing for two further Stage Gates. **RETRO-015:** a broker's office address was independently fact-verified early in the engagement, but correctly withheld from publication in a separate, later Decision Register entry until the client explicitly authorized public display — fact-verification and publish-authorization tracked throughout as two distinct decisions, not one. **RETRO-016:** a Compliance/Standards Liaison's ambiguous two-word approval reply ("move forward") to a clearance log naming several specific open items was not assumed to resolve those items; the team asked directly which scope was intended and logged both the granted approval and, by name, which flagged items were not demonstrably individually verified. Requires all page-count totals to be shown as enumerated category breakdowns, not bare numbers (SEO-Architecture Sec. 15; Development SG8/SG10 Checklists); requires "100% coverage" claims to be checked against the Sitemap's own categories, not a claiming document's internal list (SEO-Architecture Sec. 14; Development SG8 Checklist); and strengthens Governance Sec. 13.5 with the two-approval fact/publish-authorization rule and the blanket-approval-disambiguation rule, both also added to Development's SG9 Checklist.
