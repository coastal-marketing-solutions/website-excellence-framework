# CR-033 — Stage Transition Check and Stage Ledger

## What changed

A general, mechanical safeguard against skipping Stage Gates, added as best-practice rules and a ledger template:

1. **Stage Transition Check** (Governance Sec. 5.3 rule 6): before work begins in any stage, list the previous stage's Required Documents and Exit Criteria and verify each document exists at its stated path by listing the folder. A missing predecessor document or missing Input is a stop.
2. **Stage Ledger** (rule 7 and new template `_config/Stage-Ledger.md`): per-document and per-exit-criterion status, updated at every transition and session close. Makes a skipped stage visible (an unopened and a skipped stage otherwise look the same).
3. **Approvals do not close stages** (rule 8).
4. **Deviation only by `GOVERNANCE-EXCEPTION`** (rule 9), never by prose in `AGENTS.md`/`CONTEXT.md`.
5. **Universal Exit Criteria** in the Fixed Stage Gate Template (applies to every gate without editing each chapter).
6. **Sec. 1.8:** project-specific gates, prompts, and definitions of done are additive to, never substitutes for, the stage spine; must be mapped to WEF stages in the root `CONTEXT.md`.
7. `new-engagement` scaffolds the ledger with every stage's Required Documents pre-listed as "Not started".

## Why (RETRO-022)

A live engagement was driven by a project-specific initiation prompt with its own "Gate A". Strategy and research were produced and the client approved the direction; work then advanced into UX and Design while SG2–SG5 were never opened and one SG1 document was missing. Detection came only from the client asking. See RETRO-022 in Governance Sec. 15.4.
