# CR-020 — Cross-industry operating-discipline expansion *(Working Draft)*

**Status:** Pending Methodology Governance Board approval

Proposed cross-industry controls generalized from live mortgage and real-estate work: source provenance and lifecycle freshness; digital-estate and legacy preservation; one-writer capability ownership, which takes precedence over indiscriminate licensed-feature activation; secure access/environment boundaries; paid-plugin entitlement/account/site-assignment verification; third-party custom-domain/origin/access-control cutover; artifact/version/language-bound approvals; evidence-led search opportunities and bounded rank tracking; selected-page/local-landing quality gates; contextual internal links; classified link-audit triage; conversion-to-operations handoff and real destination-receipt QA; all-post-type content-as-files coverage; deterministic batch releases and rollback evidence; authoring-source versus temporary verified-mirror Tier-3 modes; environment-qualified hierarchy preflight; proportional WordPress maintenance; AI verification packets; meaningful-event/no-PII and consent QA; geographic/map integrity; representative post-release indexing verification; localization QA; LocationCard interaction/accessibility; an optional video deployment brief; and a Real Estate-specific school-information boundary. Plugin scores remain diagnostics, not KPIs; industry-specific facts remain in their Modules. **Pending formal Governance Board approval.**
