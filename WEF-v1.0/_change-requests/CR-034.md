# CR-034: Search Discovery & Local Presence Setup; handoff and Action Items Sheet cadence

**Status:** Pending Governance Board approval. Implemented in the working tree; not yet merged.
**Number note:** `CR-033` is held by an unmerged draft in `assets/` (`CR-033-DRAFT-Engagement-KB-Conformance.md`), so `CR-034` was taken to avoid a collision (next-free-number rule, `_change-requests/CONTEXT.md`).
**Origin:** the Going Live AI engagement (October 2026), where both gaps were met in practice.

## What changed

1. **Search Discovery & Local Presence Setup** (Core 07, SG11.5 Sec. 10.1). A named, ordered launch-week procedure: verify the site in the search-engine webmaster tools and submit sitemaps; enable instant indexing (IndexNow) and submit all URLs; link analytics to the SEO plugin **without** installing a second analytics tag where a consent-gated tag exists; complete the SEO plugin configuration; approve and record tracked keywords (labelled unvalidated until real query data exists); create the local business profile and claim the listings that fit; single-source the phone and email and expose them as links; security headers and single-hop redirects; record everything and queue owner-only steps. SG11 gets one checklist item and one exit criterion that the setup is prepared; SG11.5 gets a step 0, an output (an appendix to the Growth Program Plan, so the count of Required Documents is unchanged), a checklist item and an exit criterion.
2. **Handoff File and Action Items Sheet cadence** (Core 08, Sec. 2.6). Defines the division of labour between `HANDOFF.md` and the client-facing Action Items Sheet, the refresh cadence (end of every session, every Stage Gate close, after live-site changes, before go-live action, weekly while active, and at 1, 4 and 12 weeks after launch), the minimum contents of a handoff, how to process answers, and the failure modes to avoid.
3. **Stage Gate 10 and the stage trigger.** SG10 now plans the discovery setup (outputs, workflow step 4, checklist, exit criteria) and states that an engagement implemented straight from the Build Package is not complete until SG11 and SG11.5 are started. A new `start-stage` skill is the trigger: sweep mode reports every engagement's real stage and what is missing; start mode runs the Stage Transition Check (missing documents become recorded exceptions), creates the stage folder, records the Stage Ledger entry and queues owner-only steps on the Action Items Sheet.
4. **Non-gate containers.** `90-audits/`, `91-addenda/` and `92-handoffs/` are added to the KB structure as containers, not Stage Gates, numbered outside the gate sequence so they cannot collide with Stage Gate 12A/12B and exempt from the scaffold-ahead rule. They give audits, scope addenda and superseded handoffs a permanent home. `start-stage` sweep reports misplaced files and proposes moves.
5. **Starter files.** `assets/templates/engagement-KB/HANDOFF.md`; `AGENTS.md` template gains "Action items sheet" and "Handoff cadence" sections; the `new-engagement` skeleton listing includes `HANDOFF.md`.

## Why

- Search discovery and local profile setup were, until now, implicit in "SEO QA" and "indexation settings check". Engagements either skipped them or did them in an order that caused avoidable errors: analytics code installed twice or outside the consent decision; contact details hidden from crawlers by email obfuscation; a profile created under a trade name that had not been filed; sitemaps never submitted; tracked keywords chosen without data and never replaced.
- Owner questions were scattered across chat, the handoff and decision logs, which made it unclear what was waiting on whom and let the build stall on unanswered items.

## Not changed

No Stage Gate was added or renumbered. No industry-specific rule was added (the procedure is tool-agnostic; tools are named only as examples). No required-document count changed.
