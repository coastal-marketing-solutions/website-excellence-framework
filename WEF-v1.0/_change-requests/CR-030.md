# CR-030 — Implement the `master-content-workbook` skill *(Working Draft)*

**Status:** Pending Methodology Governance Board approval
**Closes:** the "skill outstanding" thread opened by CR-025 and carried in CR-027 (F-3).

## Problem

CR-025 added the Master Content Workbook as a Required Document at SG5/SG8/SG9 and named a
`.agents/skills/master-content-workbook/SKILL.md` to build it — but that skill was never written.
Reusable Templates Sec. 8.4 has carried an inline "until it exists, build it by hand" procedure
since CR-025, marked outstanding in CR-027. Separately, three references in the gate chapters
(SEO & Architecture Sec. 5; Development Sec. 5 at SG8 and SG9) pointed the workbook's per-state
detail at a non-existent `Reusable Templates Sec. 9.1 / 9.2 / 9.3` — a dangling reference the
CR-027 F-3 sweep missed.

## Change

1. **New `.agents/skills/master-content-workbook/SKILL.md`** — three modes matched to the gate:
   SKELETON (SG5), ENRICH (SG8), FINALISE (SG9). The skill decides *what goes in each sheet and
   from which upstream artifact*; it calls the `xlsx` skill for the spreadsheet mechanics. It
   builds the workbook once and extends it in place, never regenerating it. It carries the
   RETRO-013 (enumerated counts, not bare totals), RETRO-014 (check coverage against the
   Sitemap's own categories), RETRO-015 (written ≠ cleared-to-publish), and RETRO-016 (no
   blanket clearance) disciplines at the points they apply.
2. **Reusable Templates Sec. 8.4 rewritten** — remains the workbook's canonical definition, but
   now points at the skill as the way to execute it rather than an inline "build by hand"
   fallback. The "skill outstanding" language is removed.
3. **Gate references updated** — SEO & Architecture Sec. 5 (SG5) and Development Sec. 5 (SG8,
   SG9) name the skill and its mode; the three `see Sec. 9.x` dangling refs are corrected to
   `see Sec. 8.4 / the master-content-workbook skill`.
4. **AGENTS.md** — routing row added.

Additive. No Stage Gate exit criteria change (the workbook was already a Required Document at
these gates per CR-025); this makes producing it repeatable instead of ad hoc.

## Validation

- SKILL.md frontmatter present; `xlsx` skill referenced for mechanics, not reimplemented.
- No remaining `Reusable Templates Sec. 9.1/9.2/9.3` references to the workbook.
- Sec. 8.4 no longer says the skill is "not implemented" / "outstanding".
- `git diff --check` clean; links resolve.
