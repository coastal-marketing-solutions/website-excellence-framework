from three `CR-0NN-DRAFT` proposal files that CR-027–CR-029 had already superseded with

one home).

   `ARCHIVED (CR-032)` banner pointing at its live successor:

   - `CR-029-DRAFT-*` → `_change-requests/CR-029.md`

   - `WEF-Three-Engagement-Reconciliation-Crosswalk-2026-08-13.md` → `WEF-Multi-Context-Reconciliation-Protocol.md`
3. **Inbound link fix:** `_change-requests/CR-029.md`'s Board-memo link repointed to
