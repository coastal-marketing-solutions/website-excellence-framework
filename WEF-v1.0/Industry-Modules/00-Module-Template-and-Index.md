## Purpose

Every Industry Module in this library is authored to the same fixed template, for the same reason the Core Methodology's Stage Gates are all authored to the same 19-part template: consistency is what makes the framework's Module Injection Point mechanism (Core Methodology, Governance, Section 9) work reliably. A consultant or AI model that has read one Module can navigate any other Module immediately.

## The Fixed Module Template (12 Parts)

1. **Module Overview & Applicability** — What kind of client this Module fits, and where its boundaries are (including notes on when to blend with another Module)
2. **Regulatory & Compliance Landscape** — The applicable regulatory/professional-standards frameworks, licensing bodies, and known claim-risk language patterns for this vertical
3. **Persona Library** — 4-6 archetype personas typical of this vertical's audience, to be validated/refined per client at Stage Gate 1
4. **Competitive Landscape Notes** — Typical competitor archetypes, common strengths/weaknesses, and known table-stakes vs. differentiator patterns
5. **Positioning & Messaging Patterns** — Proven positioning angles and known claim-risk language for this vertical
6. **Information Architecture Patterns** — Typical sitemap structure and required page types
7. **SEO & Keyword Strategy** — Known high-value topical clusters, typical schema types, and claim-risk keyword patterns
8. **Trust Signal Requirements** — What this vertical's audience needs to see to trust a provider, and where it typically must appear
9. **Content Model & Page Types** — Required content per page type, including compliance content requirements
10. **Stage Gate Injection Map** — Quick-reference table showing exactly which Core Stage Gate consumes which Module section
11. **Module-Specific Prompt Library Additions** — Any prompt refinement beyond the generic Core Methodology prompts that is specific enough to this vertical to warrant a dedicated version
12. **Module Version History** — Versioned per Core Methodology, Governance, Section 11; reviewed at minimum annually per Section 13.7

## Blended-Module Notes

Several common blends are worth anticipating (Core Methodology, Governance, Section 1.5):

- **Real Estate (primary) + Mortgage Lending (secondary)** — a brokerage with an in-house lending arm
- **Financial Advisor (primary) + Law Firm (secondary)** — an advisory practice with embedded estate-planning legal services
- **Law Firm (primary) + Financial Advisor (secondary)** — an elder-law or estate-planning firm offering fee-based planning
- **Home Services (primary) + SaaS (secondary)** — a home services company that also licenses its own scheduling/CRM software to franchisees
- **Real Estate (primary) + Cash Home Buyer / Real Estate Investor (secondary)** — a brokerage with an in-house direct-cash-purchase arm, or an investor business expanding into traditional listing representation
- **Distressed Property Advocate (primary) + Cash Home Buyer / Real Estate Investor (secondary)** — an organization offering a distressed owner a genuine menu of paths (list traditionally, or sell directly for cash), as in the dual-site pattern documented in the Distressed Property Advocate Module's Development Note
- **Real Estate (primary) + Expired Listing Specialist (secondary)** — a generalist brokerage adding an expired-listing specialization alongside standard buy/sell service
- **Cash Home Buyer / Real Estate Investor (primary) + Probate Real Estate Investor (secondary)** — a general investor who wants dedicated probate content without probate being their sole focus (see the Probate Module's own Section 1 for when to use it standalone instead)
- **Real Estate Development (primary) + Commercial Real Estate (secondary)** — a developer with an in-house leasing and/or property-management arm handling its own completed, retained ("build-to-hold") projects
- **Commercial Real Estate (primary) + Expired Listing Specialist (secondary)** — a full-service commercial brokerage that also runs a dedicated expired-listing lead-generation program
